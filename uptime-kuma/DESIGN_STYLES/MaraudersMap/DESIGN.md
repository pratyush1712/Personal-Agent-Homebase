## Overview

Style Name: `Harry Potter Film Location Guide`

Description: A vintage-inspired design system for a Harry Potter film location guide, featuring classical typography with IM Fell Double Pica as the primary typeface, warm earth tones dominated by deep burgundy (#660000) and dark brown (#24201e), and subtle shadows that create depth without overwhelming the content. The design evokes the magical, literary atmosphere of the Harry Potter universe while maintaining excellent readability for location information.

Tags: `vintage` `literary` `cinematic` `heritage` `immersive`

Vintage literary elegance with a magical, cinematic quality. The design feels scholarly and immersive, evoking old manuscripts and heritage locations while maintaining modern usability. Dense with information but never overwhelming, creating an atmosphere of discovery and exploration.

Content-focused layout with generous whitespace around text blocks. Cards are organized in grids with consistent spacing (30px, 16px, 10px). The design uses a flexible container system that adapts to content while maintaining readability. Section spacing follows a clear rhythm based on the 4px base unit.

Uses a 4px base grid with scale: 1, 2, 2.5, 4, 6, 7.5, 8.

## Typography

- **Headline Font**: IM Fell Double Pica
- **Body Font**: IM Fell Double Pica
- **Label Font**: Open Sans
- **Code Font**: Source Sans Pro

The typography system is anchored by IM Fell Double Pica, a classical serif that evokes historical manuscripts and literary tradition. Font weights are kept minimal (400 for body, 700 for emphasis), creating hierarchy through size relationships rather than weight variation. Line heights are generous (1.7x ratio) for excellent readability. The small caps variant (IM Fell Double Pica SC) provides elegant emphasis for categories and labels. Open Sans serves as the utilitarian counterpoint for interface elements, maintaining clarity in navigation and interactive components.

## Elevation

Subtle depth created through soft shadows (rgba(0,0,0,0.5) 0px 1px 2px) and layered content cards. The design avoids heavy shadows, instead relying on color contrast and gentle elevation to create hierarchy. Modal overlays use minimal shadows for clean separation.

## Components

- **Featured Location Card**: Cards with burgundy 'FEATURED' badges, location images, titles, and metadata using classical typography
- **Consent Modal**: Clean modal dialogs with rounded corners, clear typography hierarchy, and blue action buttons
- **Navigation Header**: Top navigation with search functionality, location filters, and brand identity
- **Interactive Map**: Geographic visualization with location markers and regional filtering
- **Location Metadata**: Structured information display with icons, addresses, and contextual details

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do use IM Fell Double Pica for all content text to maintain the literary atmosphere
- Don't use heavy font weights - rely on size and color for hierarchy
- Do maintain generous line spacing (1.7x) for optimal readability
- Don't use bright, saturated colors that clash with the vintage aesthetic
- Do use the burgundy (#660000) sparingly for maximum impact on featured elements
- Don't create harsh shadows - keep elevation subtle and paper-like
- Do ensure sufficient contrast between text and warm cream backgrounds
- Don't mix too many border radius values - stick to the established scale
