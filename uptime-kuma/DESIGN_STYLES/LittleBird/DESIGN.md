## Overview

Style Name: `Warm Minimalism`

Description: Littlebird employs a sophisticated warm minimalist design with a cream background (#fffaeb), elegant serif typography (Meraki for headlines, Sohne Buch for body text), and generous spacing. The design emphasizes readability and trust through high contrast text, rounded corners, and subtle shadows on interactive elements.

Tags: `minimal` `warm` `elegant` `modern` `sophisticated`

Sophisticated and approachable with a warm, premium feel. The design balances minimalism with personality through careful typography choices and a cream-tinted color palette. High information density is managed through generous spacing and clear visual hierarchy.

Centered content with generous margins, consistent vertical rhythm based on 24px increments, and clear section separation. The layout uses a single-column approach for the hero area with symmetric spacing and careful attention to reading flow.

Uses a 4px base grid with scale: 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 15, 16, 20.

## Colors

- **Charcoal** (#1b1c15): Main headings and body text
- **Pure Black** (#000000): High contrast text and icons
- **Warm Cream** (#fffaeb): Main page background
- **White** (#ffffff): Card backgrounds and button text
- **Secondary Background** (#fff9e9): Subtle background variations
- **Dark Gray** (#222222): Secondary text and UI elements
- **Medium Gray** (#6b6b6b): Muted text and descriptions
- **Sky Blue** (#3898ec): Links and interactive elements

## Typography

- **Headline Font**: Meraki
- **Body Font**: Sohne Buch

The typography system creates clear hierarchy through size and weight rather than color variation. Meraki serif provides elegance for headings with tight letter-spacing on larger sizes (-3px on 50px), while Sohne Buch offers excellent readability for body text. Weight is used sparingly - primarily 400 weight with selective 500 and 700 for emphasis. Line-height relationships maintain consistent vertical rhythm.

## Elevation

Subtle depth is created through minimal shadow usage - primarily inset shadows on interactive elements and occasional drop shadows on cards. The design leans toward flatness with depth conveyed more through spacing and typography hierarchy than heavy shadow work.

## Components

- **Primary Button**: Dark rounded buttons with white text, 20px border radius, used for primary actions like downloads
- **Badge**: Small rounded badges with gold accent color and white background for highlighting achievements
- **Navigation**: Clean horizontal navigation with dropdown indicators and consistent spacing
- **Hero Section**: Large centered text blocks with generous vertical spacing and clear hierarchy
- **Card**: White background cards with subtle shadows and rounded corners for content grouping

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do maintain the warm cream background for brand consistency
- Don't use harsh shadows - keep elevation subtle with minimal drop shadows
- Do preserve generous spacing between sections for breathing room
- Don't mix additional font families - stick to Meraki and Sohne Buch pairing
- Do use negative letter-spacing on large Meraki headings for optical balance
- Don't compromise on contrast ratios - maintain dark text on light backgrounds
- Do keep button corner radius consistent at 20px for brand recognition
- Don't overuse the blue accent color - reserve for truly interactive elements
